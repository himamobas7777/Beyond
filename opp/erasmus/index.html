Erasmus+ for Students

European Commission · Scholarships

Support for a study period abroad, traineeships abroad, and Erasmus Mundus Joint Master Degrees, which have scholarships available. Extra funding exists for students with physical, mental or health-related conditions. The page was last updated 13 September 2022.
